Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including your email address for account creation and the financial transactions you choose to log. We do not collect sensitive banking credentials or credit card numbers.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the expense tracking service. This includes calculating your spending patterns, generating reports, and managing your account. We do not sell your personal financial data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

We use secure databases to store your information. While we implement industry-standard security measures, please be aware that no online storage is completely secure. You can request the deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not currently share data with third-party services. If we implement any analytics or cloud hosting services in the future, they will be disclosed in an updated version of this policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact our support team via the contact details provided in your account settings.
